# Mission: Operate my own VPS

## Why
I bought a VPS to learn deployment, control and monitoring, and to practice an infrastructure playbook for setting it up. I want to connect the concepts to safe, repeatable operations rather than follow unexplained commands.

## Success looks like
- Explain how the OS, containers, DNS, TLS and APIs fit together.
- Deploy a small application without unnecessarily exposing its services.
- Check health and logs, make independent backups, and demonstrate recovery in a safe environment.
- Turn a successful manual setup into a repeatable, verified playbook.

## Constraints
- Use Hostinger's VPS documentation as the provider reference.
- Start with beginner-level material (assumption authorized by “just get going”; not an assessment).
- Deliver local HTML lessons and printable references; do not change the actual VPS.

## Out of scope
- No additional exclusions were specified; this first installment does not implement a production setup.
